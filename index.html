Questions
Can Mahi Metrics change anything in my Xero?

No. We only ask for read-only access, so we can't create, edit or delete anything. You can remove our access at any time.

I don't use Xero. Can you still help?

Yes. We can work from an export from your accounting software, or from your bank statements.

What if you don't find anything?

Then that's good news, and our promise applies: if we don't find more than the cost in your first three months, you don't pay for them.

Is this financial advice?

No. We point out possible issues for you to check against your invoices and records. You decide what to act on. For tax or investment decisions, talk to your accountant.

Can I cancel?

Any time. There's no lock-in contract, and we delete your data when you leave.
