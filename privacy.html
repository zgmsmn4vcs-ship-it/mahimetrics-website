Privacy policy

Last updated: November 2026

Mahi Metrics ("we", "us") checks the financial records of businesses and organisations and prepares reports for them. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights under the New Zealand Privacy Act 2020.

What we collect

From your accounting system: when you give us read-only access to Xero (or send us an export), we read your bills, payments, invoices, account codes and contact names. We do not need, and remove automatically, addresses, phone numbers, email addresses and similar personal details.

From you directly: your name, organisation name, email address and phone number when you contact us or sign up.

How we use it

Only to prepare your reports, provide our service, contact you about it, and send invoices. We do not sell, rent or share your information with anyone for marketing, and we do not use your data to train AI models.

Access to your Xero

We only ask for read-only access. We cannot create, change or delete anything in your Xero. You can remove our access at any time, either by removing the Mahi Metrics user in Xero or by disconnecting the app in your Xero settings.

How we store and protect it

Your data is stored on encrypted computers with an encrypted backup. Access is limited to the people preparing your reports. Xero login tokens are stored securely and are never shared. If a privacy breach is likely to cause serious harm, we will tell you and the Office of the Privacy Commissioner as required by law.

Who else sees it

Nobody, except: service providers we rely on to run the business (such as our email and cloud backup providers), our accountant for our own tax and accounts, or where the law requires it. Some of these providers may store data outside New Zealand; we only use providers with appropriate protections.

How long we keep it

While you are a customer, and for up to 90 days afterwards so we can answer questions about past reports. After that we delete it, except records we must keep by law (such as our own invoices to you, kept for 7 years).

Your rights

You can ask to see the personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Email mahimetrics@outlook.com and we'll respond within 20 working days. If you're not happy with our response, you can complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner.

Our reports

Our reports point out possible issues for you to check. They are not financial, tax or legal advice.
